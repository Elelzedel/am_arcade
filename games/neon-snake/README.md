# Neon Snake

A modern arcade take on Snake. Glide a glowing neon tube through 8 handcrafted mazes, chain pellets for combos, grab bonus gems before they fade, and dash your way to the next level.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Arrows / WASD | Steer (up to 2 turns are buffered) |
| Space (hold) | Dash: move at double speed while the meter lasts |
| P | Pause |

## Scoring

- Pellet: 10 x combo (doubled while dashing)
- Combo: eat the next pellet before its gold ring runs out to raise the multiplier (up to x8)
- Bonus gem: 50-500 points; the faster you grab it, the more it is worth
- Level clear: every body segment turns into bonus points
- Extra life every 5000 points

## Levels

Eat the number of pellets shown at the start of each level to clear it. Each of the 8 mazes has its own color theme, and some have portal pairs. After level 8 the mazes repeat at a higher speed. Crashing costs a life and restarts the level at starting length, but you keep the pellets you have already eaten.

## Tips

- Use dash to reach far pellets before the combo ring runs out. The meter refills slowly.
- Tap two turns in quick succession to make tight U-turns.
- Portals keep your direction, so line up before you enter one.
